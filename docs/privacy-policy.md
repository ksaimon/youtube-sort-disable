# FocusTube Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

FocusTube helps you hide YouTube Shorts and the YouTube home feed on your device.

## Accessibility service and screen content

FocusTube uses Android's AccessibilityService API to read on-screen content only in YouTube and the selected browsers: Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, Samsung Internet, Firefox, Opera, and Vivaldi. It uses that content only to detect Shorts and the YouTube home feed, apply your hiding choices, and follow your selected feed mode.

No on-screen content is stored, logged, or sent anywhere. Content is inspected locally while the service is enabled and active. FocusTube does not create or retain a record of your account details, browsing history, search history, or screen content.

## Information stored on your device

FocusTube stores your feature choices, home-feed mode, focus-hours schedule, accessibility disclosure acceptance, and temporary pause time in private app preferences on your device. These settings are not sent to FocusTube or other parties. They are removed when you clear FocusTube's app storage or uninstall the app. FocusTube opts out of Android app backup with `android:allowBackup="false"`.

## Network access, analytics, and advertising

FocusTube declares no `INTERNET` permission and does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. FocusTube does not collect user data for transmission or transmit data. When you choose a browser redirect, FocusTube asks that browser to open the destination; the browser handles any network connection under its own privacy terms.

## Sharing and retention

FocusTube does not share user data with anyone. Screen content is used transiently for the feature and is not retained. Only your local app settings remain on the device until changed, app storage is cleared, or FocusTube is uninstalled.

## Your choices

You can turn the service off in Android Settings under Accessibility, disable FocusTube's master switch, clear the app's storage, or uninstall FocusTube. The app includes an in-app Privacy screen and instructions for disabling or uninstalling the service.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact developer link on the FocusTube Google Play listing.
